# Data licence and attribution

This repository contains **no connectome data**. Its code runs on files you download yourself from FlyWire / Codex and, for MANC and MCNS, from the Janelia dataset pages. The small tables in `results/` are derived from those data and are shared with the attribution below.

## Licence
The FlyWire FAFB v783 and BANC data products, and the Janelia MANC and MCNS releases, are published under the **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC-BY 4.0)** licence. The licence applies to each dataset's own release terms. Before you redistribute anything, check the current terms on the Codex download page for each dataset and in the FlyWire principles: https://flywire.ai/principles.html.

## Required attribution
Follow the citation requirements listed at:
- About Codex: https://codex.flywire.ai/about_codex
- About FlyWire: https://codex.flywire.ai/about_flywire
- FlyWire principles: https://flywire.ai/principles.html

At minimum, cite:
- **FAFB / FlyWire v783:** Dorkenwald et al. (2024) *Neuronal wiring diagram of an adult brain*, Nature 634:124–138. Schlegel et al. (2024) *Whole-brain annotation and multi-connectome cell typing of Drosophila*, Nature 634:139–152. FAFB EM volume: Zheng et al. (2018) Cell 174:730–743. Codex: Matsliah et al. (2024) Nature 634:153–165 (and the Codex paper listed on the About Codex page). Synapse and neurotransmitter predictions: Buhmann et al. (2021) Nature Methods; Eckstein et al. (2024) Cell.
- **BANC:** Bates et al. (2025), *Distributed control circuits across a brain-and-cord connectome*, bioRxiv (cite the version that matches the release you use).
- **MANC v1.2.1:** Takemura et al. (2024) eLife; Marin et al. (2024) eLife; Cheong et al. (2024) eLife.
- **MCNS:** Berg et al. (2025), male CNS connectome, bioRxiv (cite the version that matches the release you use).
- **LIF model the simulator re-implements:** Shiu et al. (2024) *A Drosophila computational brain model reveals sensorimotor processing*, Nature 634:210–219 (github.com/philshiu/Drosophila_brain_model).
